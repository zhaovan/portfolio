His progammatic interests revolve around creative tools, computational systems, and play. He is interested in how computers, systems, and individuals intertwine and create new affordances that push the boundaries of human capability, derive latent spaces, and reinterpret our finite understanding of the world. Ivan is drawn to permutations of forms and objects, the interaction between human and machine, and the mathematical and mechanical language of creation.

His artistic work reckons with digital, diasporic, and queer identity through nonlinear narratives, forms, and mechanics. His current practice revoles around poetry, typography, book objects, websites, and games. His work interrogates questions, such as: how do humans interrogate computational and individual agency, or the language and visualization of translation, characters, and silences? Who defines access for legibility, and the permanence and meteriality of language?

Some of his work has been exhibited in places such as _SF Public Library_, _Silver Sprocket_, _Kearny Street Workshop_, _Providence Place Library_, _Culture Hub_, _Letterform Archive_, and _Harvey Milk Photo Center_. His webart work can be found in _Taper_, _thehtml.review_,_The New River_, _Adjacent_, and _Crawlspace_. His poetry can be found in places such as _Prism_, _Foglifter_, _godz rising_, and _Kernel_.

He is grateful to have been given the opportunity to speak at conferences such as [!!con](https://bangbangcon.com/speakers.html#ivan-zhao), [Causal Islands](https://fission.codes/blog/causal-islands-la-community-edition/), and has guest critiqued at SVA and elsewhere.

Currently residing in San Francisco by way of Philadelphia, Basking Ridge, Bellevue, and Providence.
