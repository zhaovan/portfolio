Stewarded the 2022 and 2023 editions of the Reboot Student Fellowship, this zine is an amalgamation of student's work.

The student fellowship meets across different cohorts that all intersect with technology, such as media, labor, or race. Students are taught and primted to think critically about the role of technology in response to growing public issues, or policies, and produce a "piece" at the end, which is broadly defined as an essay, an interview, or a creative work.

This zine serves to commemorate the 2023 student fellowship by laying out all the work into a zine that could be printed and bound.
