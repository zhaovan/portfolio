_At the end of the earth, I find {}_ is a triptych collage poem on language, silence, and the body. Originally envisioned as a single projected installation, it has grown into a triptych of poems, each with a different particular. this specific installation debuted during New Inc's DEMO Festival in 2025, as three CRT TV's on plinths.

Comprised of a background track of dissonant chords in F Minor, the piece asks the viewer to place themselves in the cut out of the image of the work (as inspired by artists such as Diana Khoi Nguyen and Victoria Chang).

As Sheldon Gooch, curator for the exhibit writes:

> Presented as an interactive triptych, the poem takes on a visceral context as viewers' heartbeats— captured by a pulse sensor —modulate the composition and rhythm of the three texts. The interpretation of the poem thus becomes inextricable from one's bodily presence. Its form mirrors its content: nonlinear, intimate, and obscured, inviting the viewer to fill in the empty brackets with their own presence, memory, and sensation
